How to Interpret These Metrics
Demographic Parity (0.87)
A score of 1.0 means every group receives the same rate of "arrest likely" predictions. 0.87 indicates a small disparity — the most-predicted group is about 13% more likely to receive a positive prediction than the least-predicted group.
Equalized Odds (0.74)
Compares true-positive and false-positive rates across groups. A value below 0.80 signals that the model's error rates differ meaningfully by group and warrants further investigation.
Predictive Parity (0.91)
When the model says "arrest likely," it is correct at roughly the same rate for all groups, which is a positive fairness indicator.
